# Cremation 24/7 — Landing Page Documentation

## Project Overview
This is a landing page for **Cremation 24/7**, a carbon-negative cremation service operated by **Forêt de la Seconde Vie** (the parent organization).

Cremation 24/7 specializes in eco-responsible cremation services that offset their carbon footprint within 24 months and continue to capture CO₂ for decades through reforestation initiatives.

## Key Information

### Organization Structure
- **Forêt de la Seconde Vie**: Parent organization, coordinates all eco-responsible funeral services
- **Cremation 24/7**: Subsidiary dedicated exclusively to cremation services
- Both operate from Sainte-Sophie, QC

### Carbon Impact (Key Figures)
- Average tree absorbs 25 kg CO₂/year
- Over 10 years: ~250 kg CO₂ captured per tree
- Conventional cremation footprint: 50 kg CO₂
- One planted tree neutralizes conventional cremation emissions in 24 months
- Current reforestation: 7,000 trees capturing ~175 tonnes CO₂ annually

### Service Packages
1. **Conventionnelle** — $1,500 (not carbon-neutral)
2. **Carbo-négative** — $2,825 (carbon-neutral after 24 months)
3. **Héritage** — $3,674 (integrated: cremation + dispersion + artisan urn + RRQ assistance)
